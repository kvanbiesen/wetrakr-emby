Scrobbling

Very short clips are never reported. Set 0 to report everything.
  • Play, pause and stop are reported to WeTrakr as they happen and show on your profile as watching now. Who can see that is set in your privacy settings on WeTrakr.
  • An item is marked as watched when you stop after 80%. Stopping earlier saves your place on WeTrakr.
  • Marking a movie, an episode, a season or a whole series as played in Emby marks it as watched on WeTrakr too (following the two checkboxes above). Unmarking a single movie or a single episode removes that play from your WeTrakr history. Unmarking several things at once (a season, a series, a clean-up) does not touch your WeTrakr history, so a bulk change in Emby cannot wipe it.
  • Requests to WeTrakr name Emby and its version, so WeTrakr support can tell where a report came from.

Libraries WeTrakr should ignore

Watched history

Keeps what you watched in Emby and on WeTrakr together, in both directions. Importing from WeTrakr is the big one: your whole history the first time, then only what changed. It only ever marks items as played; nothing is unmarked in Emby.
Marks movies and episodes in your Emby library as played when you watched them on WeTrakr, with the date you watched them.
Catches up on plays the live updates missed (for example while the server was offline). It starts from the moment you turn it on: what you watched in Emby before is not copied over, and a sync that finds a suspiciously large number of new plays at once sends none of them. Only items WeTrakr does not already list are sent, so nothing is counted twice.
Favoriting a movie in Emby favorites it on WeTrakr right away, and a sync brings favorites from WeTrakr into Emby. Movies only - WeTrakr has no way for this plugin to address a favorited show or episode. Off by default.
Emby checks WeTrakr for what changed since the last sync and does only that. The first sync goes through your whole history.

Start over

Reads your whole WeTrakr history again, for example when a sync missed something or you now use another WeTrakr account. Nothing is unmarked: movies and episodes Emby already has as played only get their watch date refreshed.
